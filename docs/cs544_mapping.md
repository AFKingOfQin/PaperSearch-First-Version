# CS544 concepts demonstrated by PaperSearch

The project applies topics from UW–Madison's Summer 2026 Introduction to Big Data Systems in an independent paper-screening application. Each row points to something a reviewer can inspect and a result they can check. [Course repository](https://git.doit.wisc.edu/cdis/cs/courses/cs544/su26/su26mainrepo)

| Concept | Where to inspect it | Test and observed result |
|---|---|---|
| Docker, service networking and persistent volumes | [Compose profiles and health checks](../compose.yaml) | [Clean reviewer run](evidence/phase9-clean-run.md) starts two fresh volumes and reads the saved review after restarting MySQL, API and UI. |
| Kafka partitions, offsets and replay | [Producer checkpoints](../src/papersearch/publication.py) and [consumer](../src/papersearch/ingestion.py) | [Recovery harness](../tests/integration/kafka_recovery.py) interrupts either side of the SQL commit. All 15 measured stream trials drained to 384 distinct processed events. [Results](results.md) |
| SQL transactions and uniqueness | [Metadata writer](../src/papersearch/storage/metadata.py) and [screening history](../src/papersearch/storage/reviews.py) | [MySQL tests](../tests/integration/mysql_state.py) race retries and roll back interrupted writes. [Database evidence](evidence/phase2-mysql.txt) |
| Cache identity and invalidation | [Derived artifact keys](../src/papersearch/derivation.py) and [snapshot-scoped semantic reader](../src/papersearch/semantic_search.py) | [Snapshot recovery tests](../tests/integration/snapshot_recovery.py) change text or recipes. The measured update made 12 model calls with reuse and 16 with bypass. [Controlled trials](results.md) |
| Concurrency across threads and processes | [SQL lease ownership](../src/papersearch/storage/jobs.py) and [simultaneous HTTP saves](../scripts/review_demo.py) | Four retries of one request return a single history ID. Four independent writes preserve four history rows. One, two and three ingestion workers achieved median rates of 26.13, 29.91 and 36.03 events/s. [Clean run](evidence/phase9-clean-run.md) · [Worker trials](results.md) |
| PyArrow, Parquet and column projection | [Snapshot schemas](../src/papersearch/snapshot_format.py) and [format comparison](../scripts/check-spark-results.py) | At 100k rows, the identical projected query took median 0.673 s in Parquet and 1.844 s in CSV. The verifier compared every row. [Batch measurements](evidence/phase7-batch.md) |
| Spark DataFrames, SQL and execution plans | [Spark merge](../src/papersearch/batch/spark_merge.py) and [sequential reference](../src/papersearch/batch/reference_merge.py) | All three trials agreed on all winners and classifications. The 100k merge took median 2.967 s sequentially, 13.941 s with a warm JVM and 24.908 s including startup. [Plans](evidence/phase7-plans.txt) · [Reproduction](spark-batch.md) |
| Failure recovery and checkpointing | [Offset handling](../src/papersearch/ingestion.py) and [snapshot publication](../src/papersearch/snapshots.py) | Crashes leave a retryable delivery or the previous validated publication. The full metadata run resumed and retained the original review. [Snapshot faults](evidence/phase4-snapshots.txt) · [Capacity readback](evidence/phase8-capacity.json) |
| Experimental design | [Controlled workload driver](../scripts/controlled-experiments.py) and [evidence verifier](../scripts/summarize-experiments.py) | Across matched outputs, median publication took 16.13 s for rebuilding and 8.60 s for updating. The report separates the three-trial comparisons from the single full-capacity observation. [Timings and scope](results.md) |
